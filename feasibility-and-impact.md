# Feasibility and Expected Impact

## Prototype feasibility

A student prototype can be developed using free/open-source software and clearly labelled sample data. No special hardware is required for the initial software demonstration.

## Potential beneficiaries

- Authorised investigation and cybercrime-response teams
- Banks and financial institutions, subject to approved collaboration
- Citizens, through the broader goal of improving awareness and response
- Organisations responsible for monitoring and reviewing complaint patterns

## Expected benefits

- Easier visual review of area-level patterns
- Better organisation of complaint trends by area and time
- A possible aid for prioritising human verification
- A clear dashboard view for demonstrations and discussion

These are intended benefits, not measured outcomes. They require validation before any real-world claim is made.
