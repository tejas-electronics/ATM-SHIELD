# Problem Statement

**Reference:** SIH 2026 – SIH26184  
**Ministry / Department:** Ministry of Home Affairs – I4C  
**Theme:** Cybersecurity

The project addresses the challenge of examining ATM-related cybercrime complaint patterns across locations and time. A map-based view of area-level patterns may help authorised teams identify locations that warrant additional attention.

This repository does not reproduce or claim additional official problem-statement wording beyond the project information supplied by the team. Refer to the official SIH problem page for the authoritative statement: https://sih2026.vuce.in/ps/SIH26184
