ATM SHIELD

Predicting High-Risk ATM Withdrawal Areas

ATM SHIELD is a proposed area-level risk-awareness system that explores complaint patterns across locations and time to help authorised teams identify areas that may need closer attention.

Open Interactive Demo Open Learning App Project Documentation

Prototype notice: The current interactive demo uses fictional sample data. It is not connected to live ATM, bank, police, or cybercrime systems. A risk score is only an indicator for review, never proof of wrongdoing.
