# Project Overview

## ATM SHIELD – Predicting High-Risk ATM Withdrawal Areas

ATM SHIELD is a proposed software-based, area-level risk-awareness concept for SIH 2026 problem statement SIH26184. It explores whether cybercrime-related complaint patterns across locations and time can help authorised teams identify areas that may need closer review.

### Main objective
Present area-level risk indicators in an understandable map/dashboard so authorised teams can prioritise further verification.

### Scope boundary
The concept estimates risk for an area and time period. It does not identify a suspect, establish that a crime occurred, or replace investigation and human judgement.

### Prototype status
The supplied HTML pages are demonstration interfaces using fictional sample data. They do not connect to live ATM, bank, police, or cybercrime systems.
