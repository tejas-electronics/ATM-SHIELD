THE IDEA IN ONE MINUTE

What is ATM SHIELD?

ATM SHIELD is an AI-based platform that predicts areas where suspicious ATM cash withdrawals may be more likely.

It studies cybercrime complaint patterns, estimates area-level risk and displays the result on a map for authorised teams to review.

THE PROBLEMTeams need help identifying locations that may need attention.
OUR SOLUTIONUse data patterns and machine learning to estimate risk.
THE OUTPUTA colour-coded map, trends and review alerts.

Remember this sentence

“ATM SHIELD uses AI to predict high-risk ATM withdrawal areas and displays them on a map, helping cybercrime teams take action earlier.”

1. DATA INPUTStart with synthetic or approved anonymised complaint statistics.
2. PREPROCESSClean, check and organise the records so the model can analyse them.
3. ML ENGINEUse patterns in the data to estimate risk levels.
4. GIS MAPShow estimated risk by area using map colours.
5. DASHBOARDShow filters, trends and alerts for authorised review.

Technology stack

Important limitation

A risk score is only an analytical warning signal. It is not proof of crime. Human review is essential, and demo results using sample data must be labelled as simulated.
