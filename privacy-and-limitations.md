# Privacy, Safety, and Limitations

## Data and privacy
- Use only authorised and lawfully obtained datasets.
- Minimise personal information and protect any sensitive records.
- Apply access controls and appropriate retention practices.
- Do not publish real personal, banking, or complaint records in this repository.

## Prediction limitations
- Incomplete or changing complaint data can affect estimates.
- A model may produce false positives or false negatives.
- Geographic differences in reporting can distort apparent patterns.
- A high score is not proof of crime and must not be treated as an accusation.

## Current demo limitations
- The demo uses fictional sample areas, scores, and complaint counts.
- Its generated alerts are illustrative only.
- It has no live data connection or operational alert delivery.
- No validated model accuracy is claimed.

Any future operational use would require official approval, security review, privacy safeguards, representative data, rigorous evaluation, and human oversight.
