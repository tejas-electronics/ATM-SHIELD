# Proposed Solution

ATM SHIELD proposes an area-level risk-awareness workflow:

1. Ingest authorised complaint records or development sample data.
2. Clean and standardise the data.
3. Group records by relevant area and time period.
4. Analyse patterns and estimate an area-level risk score.
5. Categorise the score as low, medium, or high.
6. Show the result on a map and dashboard.
7. Provide alerts or trend summaries for authorised review.
8. Keep a human verification step before operational decisions.

The system is intended to support prioritisation, not automatically accuse people or make enforcement decisions.
